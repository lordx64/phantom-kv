# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in phantom-kv a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, and generous when correcting: this is a research repo, and
  every claim here is supposed to be challenged by evidence, not by status.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the quality of the research.
- No harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or publication of others'
  private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported through a GitHub issue
flagged "conduct" or privately via a GitHub Security Advisory. Maintainers
will review and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and contributions that violate this Code, and will communicate reasons when
appropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
